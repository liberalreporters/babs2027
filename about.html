PROFILE

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade is a businessman and community-focused public figure from Eruwa, Ibarapa East, Oyo State.

His public-information platform is built around the FOCUS framework — Forward-thinking Opportunities for Community Unity and Sustainability — and a stated interest in practical development, education, youth opportunity, enterprise and community engagement.
