About Babatunde

A neutral overview designed to distinguish documented information from details awaiting independent confirmation.

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade, popularly associated with the name BABS, is presented through the lenses of leadership, professional life, community interests and public service.

The portal is a factual public-information resource. Credentials, appointments, political status, statistics and other details requiring documentary confirmation should be verified before publication.

Editorial standard

This website does not present unverified claims as established facts, and does not make endorsements, election predictions or rankings.
