About BABS 2027

BABS 2027 is an annual gathering focused on journalism, media freedom, and public engagement. We bring together reporters, editors, and media partners to share knowledge, skills, and forward-looking strategies.

Our Mission

We support independent reporting and strengthen press ecosystems through training, resources, and accessible events.

Organizers

The event is organized by the Liberal Reporters collective — a group of journalists and advocates committed to open information and accountability.
