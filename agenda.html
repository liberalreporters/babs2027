Public Priorities

Forward-thinking Opportunities for Community Unity and Sustainability.

THE FRAMEWORK

FOCUS connects opportunity with practical community priorities.

The framework is organised around four broad areas. These pages describe the stated areas of interest and should be read as public-policy priorities rather than a claim that every proposed outcome has already been delivered.

01

Education & Human Capital

Support for learning, student development, skills acquisition and initiatives that strengthen human capital.

  • Education support
  • Skills development
  • Youth learning opportunities
  • Human-capital investment
02

Youth & Opportunity

Creating clearer pathways for young people to build skills, pursue enterprise and participate in community development.

  • Enterprise opportunities
  • Skills and employability
  • Youth participation
  • Mentorship and development
03

Community Development

Attention to practical community needs and initiatives that can contribute to local development.

  • Local infrastructure
  • Community initiatives
  • Inclusive engagement
  • Needs-based development
04

Enterprise & Sustainability

Encouraging productive economic activity while keeping development focused on durable and responsible outcomes.

  • Small-business activity
  • Property and enterprise
  • Responsible development
  • Long-term sustainability

ENGAGEMENT

Public priorities benefit from public input.

Questions, observations and community perspectives can be submitted through the contact page for consideration by the relevant office or team.
